Overview

01

HEAT ALARM ("we", "our", or "the app") is a heat-stress alerting application. It is designed to calculate the Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI) in real time and alert outdoor workers, delivery riders, and farmers when conditions become dangerous.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, why it collects it, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By installing or using HEAT ALARM, you agree to the practices described in this document.

✅

Our core promise: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Data collected is used exclusively to deliver life-saving heat-stress alerts to you.

📊

Data We Collect

02

UTCI Alert is built with a minimal-data philosophy. We collect only what is strictly necessary to compute your local heat-stress index and deliver personalized safety advice.

Data CategoryWhat Is CollectedStorageRequired?
Location GPS latitude & longitude (used on-device only for UTCI computation) On-Device Required
City Name Reverse-geocoded city/region name derived from your coordinates On-Device Required
User Profile Display name, email, and occupational role (worker / rider / farmer) entered during onboarding On-Device (Hive) Required

ℹ️

Your precise GPS coordinates are processed entirely on your device to fetch weather data. Raw coordinates are never stored permanently — only the resolved city/region name is saved locally for display purposes.

App Permissions

03

UTCI Alert requests the following Android permissions. We use only what is necessary for core functionality — no camera, contacts, microphone, or file storage access is ever requested.

📍
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
High-precision GPS required for hyperlocal UTCI calculations. Used only when the app is open (foreground only).
🗺️
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Network-based approximate location — used as a fallback when GPS is unavailable.
🌐
INTERNET
Required to fetch live weather data from Open-Meteo and geocoding data from BigDataCloud.
📳
VIBRATE
Tactile haptic feedback when a heat-stress alert fires, ensuring you notice it even in noisy environments.
⚠️

You may deny location permission at any time via Android Settings. Without location access, UTCI Alert cannot compute your local heat-stress index; however, you may still manually search for a city.

Data Storage & Retention

05

UTCI Alert stores your data primarily on your own device using Hive — a lightweight offline database. The app is designed to function fully offline once initial data is cached.

DataWhere StoredRetention
User ProfileOn-device (Hive user_box)Until you uninstall or delete your profile
UTCI Status & Weather CacheOn-device (Hive status_box)Overwritten on every app session refresh
App SettingsOn-device (Hive settings_box)Until you uninstall the app
FCM Device Token (if used)Our backend serverUntil you unregister or uninstall the app
🛡️

No cloud account required. Your profile and heat-stress history never leave your device unless you explicitly share it. We have no access to your Hive-stored data.

Third-Party Services

06

UTCI Alert integrates with the following third-party services solely to provide weather and location data. These services may process your IP address and/or coordinates in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Open-Meteo Weather API Free, open-source weather API providing temperature, humidity, wind speed, and weather codes for UTCI computation. Privacy Policy ↗
Open-Meteo Geocoding City name search for manual city selection. Sends only the typed city name — no personal data transmitted. Privacy Policy ↗
BigDataCloud Reverse Geocoding Converts GPS coordinates to a human-readable city/country name. Coordinates are sent over HTTPS; no API key required. Privacy Policy ↗
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Optional push notification delivery for heat alerts. Only a device token is shared — no personal profile data is sent. Privacy Policy ↗
ℹ️

All network requests use HTTPS (TLS). We do not share your name, email, or occupational role with any of the above services. These APIs receive only what is technically necessary: coordinates or a city name.

Data Security

09

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS / TLS encryption on all network requests to weather and geocoding APIs.
  • On-device storage only — your profile and UTCI history never transit through our servers.
  • No persistent location logging — GPS coordinates are processed in-memory and discarded after each weather fetch.
  • No cloud sync — the app functions fully offline with no background data uploads.
  • No third-party analytics SDK — we do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any advertising network.
⚠️

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your Android OS and UTCI Alert up to date.
